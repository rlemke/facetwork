How stocks are chosen

  1. Hard filters. Price above $5, 20-day average dollar volume above $10M, and at least a year of history. These exclude names where the factor math is meaningless.
  2. Factor screen. 12-1 momentum, 3-month return, 6-month strength relative to the benchmark, proximity to the 52-week high, minus a volatility penalty. Each is z-scored across the survivors and winsorized.
  3. Analyst consensus. Recommendation mean and target upside for the top 50, scored only where coverage is thick enough to mean anything.
  4. Optional LLM re-rank of the top 25, with a written reason per ticker.

The complete ranked list is saved — including the names that were not picked — so you can check afterwards how the runners-up performed.
